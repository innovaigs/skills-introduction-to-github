Our Initiatives

At InnovAI, we are committed to developing and executing projects that bring AI education to a wider audience. Our projects range from interactive learning platforms to community workshops, all designed to be engaging, accessible, and impactful.

Project Alpha: Interactive AI Learning Platform

Description: Developing an interactive online platform where users can learn fundamental AI concepts through hands-on exercises and simulations. The platform will feature modules on machine learning, neural networks, and natural language processing.

Status: In Planning

AI Literacy Workshops

Description: A series of workshops designed for beginners to understand the basics of Artificial Intelligence, its applications in daily life, and its ethical considerations. These workshops will be conducted both online and in local communities.

Status: Ongoing
